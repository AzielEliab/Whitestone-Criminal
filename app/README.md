# Whitestone Criminal

Ephemeral pro se criminal-procedure advisor. Session-only memory; wipe on close. Allowlisted court, public-defender, and self-help research. Uploads stay on this computer. No case exports. No third-party language-model APIs.

Educational. A public defender, or a lawyer you hire, represents you.

Author: Aziel Eliab.

## Open

Unzip this folder and open `index.html` in a browser. Notes stay in that tab until you press Wipe notes or close the tab.

The research links are the pages listed in `logic.js`. This copy does not add other sites.
